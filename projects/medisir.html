MediSir POS

TECHNICAL CASE STUDY

Advanced pharmacy management SaaS — the most technically complete system I’ve built.

The Problem

Small and medium pharmacies in Indonesia still rely on manual record-keeping. They need a modern, cloud-based POS system that handles inventory, prescriptions, multi-cashier operations — without expensive enterprise pricing.

What I Built

A production-grade SaaS with deep technical architecture beyond typical CRUD apps.

  • ✓Full POS with barcode scanning, multi-payment, thermal receipt + WhatsApp delivery
  • ✓Multi-tenant with PostgreSQL RLS and cashier invitation system
  • ✓Realtime inventory sync via Supabase Realtime
  • ✓Digital prescription module with validity control
  • ✓Stock audit logs, CSV import/export, Recharts financial reports

The Result & Decision to Pause

The MVP was completed with production-grade architecture. Development paused due to regulatory requirements in Indonesian healthcare. Not a technical failure — a strategic business decision.

Stack

React + TS Vite Supabase PostgreSQL + RLS Zustand Recharts Tailwind CSS
